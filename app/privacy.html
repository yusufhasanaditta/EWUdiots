Privacy Policy

Effective 28 October 2026 · Applies to the EWUdiots Android app, web app (PWA) and Chrome extension.

In short: we only handle the academic information you choose to bring in. We never ask for your portal password, and we never collect your phone number, address, national ID, date of birth or blood group. Your ID card photos, which you add to sign in, are private to your own account. There are no ads, no trackers, and we never sell data.

Who we are

EWUdiots is an independent app made by students for students of East West University (EWU), Dhaka. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to East West University. Contact: civicframe01@gmail.com.

What we never collect

  • Your EWU portal password or login session.
  • Phone number, present or permanent address, national ID, unique ID, date of birth, blood group, marital status or your portal photo. The app and extension are built to skip these even when they appear on a portal page. (ID card photos you take yourself are handled as described above.)
  • Location, contacts, advertising ID or browsing history.

The Chrome extension

The extension runs only on portal.ewubd.edu pages and only when you press Sync. It reads the visible academic tables on that page, keeps them in your browser, and hands them to the EWUdiots app you have open. It never reads form fields, cookies or passwords, and sends nothing anywhere by itself.

Service providers

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and file storage for signed-in users. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
  • Anthropic: reads screenshots when you choose Smart scan. Screenshots are processed under Anthropic's commercial terms and are not used to train models.
  • Google Fonts: serves the app's typeface.

We don't use analytics or advertising SDKs.

How long we keep data

On-device data stays until you clear it in the app or uninstall. Cloud data stays until you delete your account. Smart-scan usage counters (date only, for the daily limit) are deleted with your account.

Your choices and rights

  • Export: Account & settings → Export my data.
  • Delete: Account & settings → Delete my account. This permanently removes your account, backup, reviews and shared files. You can also request deletion here.
  • Report content: use Report on any review or file. Three reports hide it automatically until reviewed.

Children

EWUdiots is for university students and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and tell you in the app.
