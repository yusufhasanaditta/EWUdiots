Delete your EWUdiots account

In the app (instant)

  1. Open EWUdiots and go to Account & settings.
  2. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

This permanently deletes your account, cloud backup (profile, routine, grades, tasks, fees), your faculty reviews and every file you shared in the study vault. It can't be undone.

Without the app

Email civicframe01@gmail.com from your @std.ewubd.edu address with the subject "Delete my EWUdiots account". We delete everything within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is kept

Nothing linked to you. Anonymous timetable rows (course, section, time, room) and faculty directory entries you helped add stay, because they contain no personal data and other students rely on them.
