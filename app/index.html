FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is this the official EWU app?

No. EWUdiots is an independent app made by students. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to East West University. Always confirm important information on the official portal and notice board.

Where do the notices and faculty details come from?

From EWU's own public pages: the notice board at ewubd.edu and each department's faculty pages. Every notice and profile links back to the original. Faculty members can ask us to update or remove a listing.

How accurate is the 3D map?

Blocks, floor counts, lift lobbies and links between blocks come from the official campus map, to within about 3 metres. Room positions are worked out from the room number and EWU's office lists, and each one shows how sure we are. Students can add rooms and floor plans to make it better.

Is it free?

Yes. Every feature is free.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works on your device. Signing in with your EWU email adds backup, sync across devices, reviews, the shared study vault and the Chrome extension.

How do I delete my data?

App → Profile & Settings → Delete account. Or see delete account.
